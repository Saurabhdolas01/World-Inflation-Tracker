Data sources & methodology

Inflation, GDP growth, unemployment, FX rate, current account: World Bank Open Data API, annual figures, for every country the World Bank reports on.

Monthly trend (Compare view): IMF International Financial Statistics (IFS), CPI index converted to year-over-year %, for a set of major economies.

News (Country Detail view): Google News RSS, refreshed daily.

Economic data refreshes monthly via a scheduled job. Figures can lag the current date by a year or more for countries that report infrequently.
